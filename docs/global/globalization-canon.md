# SUFU Global Canon

SUFU is a global product from the architecture level. Zimbabwe is the launch market, not the architectural boundary.

## Global dimensions
- Geography: World → Country → Region/State/Province → City → District → Locality → Neighbourhood.
- Location input: GPS, manual selection, map search and geocoding.
- Privacy: exact, approximate or area/service-radius location.
- Time: UTC storage; local timezone presentation.
- Currency: ISO 4217-style codes; original transaction/listing currency remains authoritative.
- Languages: locale-keyed translations; English, ChiShona and isiNdebele are launch languages; additional African and global languages can be added without restructuring UI.
- Direction: RTL-ready.
- Search: natural-language queries may contain location, language and economic intent.
- Economic scope: local, remote, hybrid, national and cross-border.
- Identity: international phone numbers and country-aware verification.
- Providers: country-specific payment, verification, tax/legal and commerce adapters.
- Data boundary: country configuration must not leak provider credentials or private location data to clients.

## Product principle
**Global architecture. Local relevance.**

A user should experience SUFU as a service that understands where they are, what they need and how their market works, without the product being structurally tied to one country.

## Expansion rule
Adding a country should primarily mean adding configuration, geography data, localization, compliant provider adapters and market policies—not rewriting the marketplace core.
